# Micromobility Crash Prevalence – Class Project

## Description
As part of a graduate-level project, I partnered with another student to investigate the relationship between micromobility-related crashes (MRCs), high-risk behaviors, and traffic safety knowledge among college students at a large metropolitan university. Using survey responses from 379 students, we conducted data wrangling and statistical analysis in SAS. We found no significant link between risky behaviors and crash involvement; however, students who experienced a crash reported higher traffic safety knowledge. International students and on-campus residents had higher crash rates, indicating potential disparities in safety outcomes. We submitted our findings to the Journal of American College Health, where the manuscript is currently under review.
